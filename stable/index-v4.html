What this model is, and what it leaves out

A model, not a trial

These are results of a computer model. They show what follows from the assumptions above, and they change when the assumptions change. They are not measurements of what lamps do in a real care home, and they say nothing about any one person's health.

We know of no completed randomised trial showing that far-UVC lamps reduce infections in care homes. Whether they do is an open question that this model cannot settle.
