# The Air We Share (And Nothing Else)

A song for Suno that does the job of the Explanation film: every caption in `web/captions.json` (figures as of
20 November 2026) is sung, in the film's order. Numbers are spelled out so they are sung as written.

## Style of music

Short form (fits the 200-character box):

```
dance-punk, nu-disco, 122 BPM, rubbery bass, cowbell, four-on-the-floor, synth stabs, deadpan talk-sung female lead, crisp diction, shouted gang vocals, spoken breakdown, key change finale
```

Long form (newer Suno versions):

```
Dance-punk meets nu-disco at 122 BPM. Rubbery picked bass riff, cowbell, tight four-on-the-floor drums, clean
guitar stabs, bright analog synth arpeggios. Lead is a deadpan, talk-sung female voice with very crisp diction,
every number clearly pronounced. A crowd of gang vocals shouts the numbers in brackets back at her. Choruses are
huge and chanted. Half-time spoken breakdown with the drums out, then a key change up into a euphoric final
chorus. Ends on a dry spoken line with the music cut. Punchy, modern, dry mix.
```
